Where the water went

Four source paths

Upstairs bathroom

  1. Source at the fixture or supply
  2. Down through the ceiling cavity
  3. Along joists
  4. Wall base two rooms away
  5. Subfloor under flooring that still looks fine

What looks fine but isn’t: Ceiling two rooms over · Wall base below the source · Subfloor under intact flooring

Kitchen

  1. Dishwasher, fridge line, or sink supply
  2. Into the kick space and cabinet floor
  3. Through the subfloor
  4. Into the ceiling of the room below if stacked

What looks fine but isn’t: Cabinet backs that look dry · Adjacent pantry toe-kick · Ceiling below

Roof

  1. Penetration or valley
  2. Attic insulation
  3. Ceiling drywall
  4. Exterior wall cavity on the downhill side

What looks fine but isn’t: Insulation that holds water out of sight · Top plates · Windows below the leak

Basement floor

  1. Slab or crawl
  2. Up the bottom plate
  3. Into the first wall cavity
  4. Under finished flooring at the edge

What looks fine but isn’t: Bottom plates · Carpet tack strip · The 'dry' corner on the other side of the room

Questions

Where is the main shutoff?

On a detached house it is often at the front wall or in a mechanical closet. In a condo, call the building before you hunt for a riser. Use the shutoff finder on First Hour.

The pipe burst upstairs. Why is the room below wet?

Water travels through ceiling cavities and along joists. The room that looks fine is often the one that needs the meter.

Does Vancouver get freeze bursts?

Less often than the Interior, but unheated garages, crawl spaces, and North Shore hillside lines still fail in cold snaps. We treat them as supply-line losses.

Should I use a shop vac?

Only on clean water, and never as a substitute for cavity extraction. Never on sewage. Photograph first.

Will you repair the pipe?

We stop the damage and dry. Pipe repair may be us or a plumber depending on the line. Rebuild is quoted after dry-out.

Is a burst pipe Category 1?

A potable supply line starts as Category 1. If it sits, it degrades. Time on the damage clock is the honest version of urgency.
