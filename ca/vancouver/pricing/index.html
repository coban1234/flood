Three commitments

No surprise line items.

Every charge that can appear on your invoice is on this page. If it is not listed here, you will not be billed for it. No fuel surcharge. No contractor fee. No administrative add-on.

Equipment billing capped at five days.

Drying equipment is billed to the day your readings hit target, capped at five days. If your building needs longer, we show you the readings and you approve the extension before day six.

A written scope before rebuild.

Emergency mitigation proceeds immediately. Everything after it is authorised in writing before work starts. Rebuild is quoted per property after dry-out, not from this card.

Questions

Are these the full rates?

Every charge that can appear on a mitigation invoice is on this page. Rebuild is quoted after dry-out. Insurance programme work uses insurer rates, not this card.

Why is overnight more?

Our overnight and weekend rates are not discounted. Someone has to be awake, insured, and equipped at 3am, and that costs what it costs. We would rather charge properly for nights than promise a cheap one we cannot staff.

Do rates change by city?

Not inside Metro Vancouver. Burnaby and Richmond use this card. Response time changes by tier, not the price.

What is the equipment cap?

Five days without written approval. If readings are still above target, we show you and you approve day six.

Is there a fuel surcharge?

No. No contractor fee. No administrative add-on.

Can I pay out of pocket?

Yes. See without a claim. This card is for that work.

Still wet? Call.

We answer 24/7 — a person, not a service. If you would rather not talk, tell us what happened in four taps and we will call you.
